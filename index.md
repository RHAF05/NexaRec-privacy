# NexaRec Privacy Policy



Last updated: October 5, 2026

NexaRec is a Windows desktop application published by RHAF. It records screen content and, when enabled by the user, microphone audio, system audio and camera content. Contact: rharife@hotmail.com.

## Recording and device access

NexaRec processes the display, window or screen region you select. When you enable microphone, system audio or camera features, it processes the corresponding input to provide previews and recordings. Screen content can include personal information from other applications. Select your recording source and enabled inputs carefully.

The current application processes recordings on your computer and saves files in the output folder configured in the application. It does not provide an automatic upload or cloud storage service. If you choose a folder synchronized by OneDrive or another service, that service may upload the files according to its own settings and privacy policy.

## Settings, history and logs

NexaRec stores settings, recording history and diagnostic logs locally. These may include recording file paths, timestamps, device selections, capture information and error details. These files are used to restore preferences, show recent recordings and diagnose problems. Windows may redirect the app's local data into its package-specific storage when installed from Microsoft Store.

The current application has no built-in advertising, usage analytics or automatic telemetry upload. It does not require a NexaRec account to record. Microsoft Store and Windows operate under Microsoft's own privacy terms and may process installation, update or diagnostic information independently of NexaRec.

## Support

If you email rharife@hotmail.com, the support mailbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request. Do not send recordings, logs or screenshots containing private information unless needed for your request and reviewed by you. Email is handled by the email providers involved under their own terms.

## Your controls and retention

You control when to start and stop recording, which supported inputs to enable, and where recording files are saved. You can manage device access through Windows privacy settings.

Recording files remain until you delete or move them. You can delete local settings, history and log files while NexaRec is closed. Removing a history entry does not itself delete the recording file. Uninstalling the application does not guarantee deletion of recordings saved outside its package data. For questions about information you sent to support, contact rharife@hotmail.com.

## Changes

This policy will be updated if NexaRec's data practices change. The updated date will identify the latest policy.
